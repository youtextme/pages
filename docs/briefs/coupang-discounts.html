What “90% off” claims actually mean

Coupang runs several named sale events. In 2025, the company’s own newsroom described Wow Big Sale as up to 70% off across 14 categories (July 7–13, 2025). Wow Week (Aug 18–24, 2025) also capped at 70%. Fashion-specific Kupase (쿠패세) ran June 16–29, 2025 with up to 80% on 500+ brands.

No verified 90% platform-wide sale found

Across Coupang newsroom releases and Korean press recaps searched for this brief, 80% is the highest event ceiling cited for fashion (Kupase). Individual SKUs may show higher percentage badges in-app, but those require per-item verification — see Section 3.

A July 2026 Women’s Fashion Festival (through July 12) offered tiered coupons (₩5,000 off ₩50,000+; ₩15,000 off ₩100,000+) rather than a flat 90% headline.

How to spot inflated “was” prices

  • Cross-check the percentage — if the badge says 90% but Coupang’s own event page caps at 80%, screenshot both.
  • Compare outside Coupang — check the brand’s official store or Naver Shopping price history for the same SKU.
  • Watch coupon stacking — Wow-member coupons (₩5,000 off ₩50,000+) change the paid price but not always the displayed “% off” badge.
  • Rocket Wow vs non-member — instant discounts in Kupase guide are member-specific; the badge may assume Wow pricing.

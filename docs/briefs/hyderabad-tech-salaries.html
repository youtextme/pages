What ₹3–6 Cr means

Indian tech compensation is usually quoted as CTC / total compensation (TC) per year, mixing base salary, RSU/stock vesting (annualized), and bonus. ₹3 crore = ₹30,000,000/year (~₹25 lakh/month gross components combined). ₹6 crore = ₹60,000,000/year.

Social posts claiming “₹3 Cr offers in Hyderabad” often conflate one-time grant value, USD offers, or outlier self-reports with steady-state TC. This brief uses Levels.fyi self-reported medians (last updated May–Aug 2026 on cited pages).

Who might approach ₹3 Cr — and who will not

Verdict: ₹3–6 Cr/year TC is not a mainstream Hyderabad big-tech band for standard software engineering tracks in public compensation datasets as of this brief.

  • Below ₹3 Cr but solid: Google L6 (~₹2 Cr), Microsoft 66–67 (~₹1.9–2.5 Cr), Amazon L6 (~₹1.2 Cr) — these are senior/staff-adjacent roles requiring many years of experience.
  • ₹3 Cr threshold: No median on cited Levels.fyi Hyderabad pages reaches ₹30M. Approaching it would require unreported Principal/Distinguished (L7+ / 68+ / E6+) offers, exceptional refresh grants, or USD-denominated packages — none verified here.
  • ₹6 Cr: No public Levels.fyi median for Hyderabad SWE approaches ₹60M. Treat viral claims as [unverified] unless backed by redacted offer letters in curated datasets.
  • Hyderabad vs Bengaluru: City premium exists but is modest (JobsByCulture aggregate table lists Hyderabad mid-level ~₹16–28L vs Bangalore ~₹18–30L) — not a 3× multiplier.

How to use this data

  • Anchor expectations to level mapping (Google L5 vs Amazon L5 vs Microsoft 63 — not the same seniority).
  • Compare TC components separately: base, stock/RSU vest schedule, sign-on, bonus.
  • Cross-check multiple sources: Levels.fyi, peer offers on Blind [unverified], and company filings for executive pay only (not individual IC offers).
  • If targeting ₹3 Cr+, plan for staff/principal scope, international transfer, or leadership comp — not default Hyderabad mid-senior SWE.
